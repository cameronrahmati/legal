SuppStack Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Last updated: September 23, 2026

SuppStack ("we", "us", "I") is operated by Cameron Rahmati, a sole proprietor doing business as SuppStack, in the State of California, United States. As a sole proprietor I am personally the data controller for the information described here. Contact: cameron@sentic.io.

The short version

You give us health information so we can build you a supplement routine. We use it for that, and for understanding how the app performs. We do not sell it. We do not run ads. We are not a covered entity under HIPAA, and SuppStack is not a medical service.

Your health answers are saved on your phone. When you generate a routine they are sent to our server, which builds the routine and keeps a copy of your answers and the routine for three days. Coming back to finish uses the copy on your phone, which is kept for 30 days. If you subscribe, we keep your answers and your routine for as long as you have an account so they can be restored on a new phone and updated later. If you do not subscribe, the server copies are deleted after three days.

What we collect

Health profile you provide during the consultation. Your goals, symptoms, sleep and wake times, stress level, diet, lifestyle habits, biological sex, age, height, weight, medications, diagnosed conditions, family history, pregnancy status, and current supplements. Every question except the required safety questions can be skipped or answered "prefer not to say".

This profile is stored on your device. When you generate a routine it is also stored in our database under the retention terms below, and sent to the service described under "How your health data is used" (re-sent if an attempt fails).

Bloodwork photos or PDFs, if you upload them. Optional. The file is sent to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API to read the lab values and returns them for you to check and confirm. Our server does not store the file. Anthropic processes it under its API data policy, which prohibits training on it and sets its own limited retention period (see anthropic.com/privacy). The values you confirm become part of your health profile and are handled as described above. You can decline this step with no effect on your routine.

Account data. An anonymous account id is created the first time you open the app, so that routine generation has a verifiable identity before you sign in. If you sign in with Apple or email, we store the email address and authentication tokens. All of this is held by Supabase, our database and authentication provider.

Purchase data. Subscription status and plan, via RevenueCat and Apple. We never see your payment card.

Your routine and daily check-offs. The routine the app builds for you, including its safety note, which may name a medication you entered, and each day's record of which supplements you marked as taken. Stored on your phone and in our database so they can be restored if you reinstall or change phones.

Usage analytics. Screen views, funnel progress, and feature usage, via Mixpanel, tied to a random per-install identifier that is not your account id. The events are: Onboarding Started, Onboarding Screen Viewed, Onboarding Completed, Routine Generation Started, Routine Generated, Routine Generation Failed, Teaser Viewed, Paywall Viewed, Paywall Tier Selected, Purchase Started, Purchase Completed, Purchase Cancelled, Purchase Failed, Win Back Viewed, Bloodwork Upload, Email Capture Viewed, Email Capture Submitted, Soft Exit Viewed, and Supplement Logged. Their properties are counts, screen ids, plan names, and error messages. Analytics events never contain your health answers, your email, or your bloodwork. If you abandon the paywall and give us your email for a findings summary, the email goes to our database, not to analytics.

Identifiers. Three identifiers exist and they are not joined. Your account id is a random id Supabase assigns when the app first opens; it labels your health profile, routine, and check-offs in our database, and we give the same id to RevenueCat so your subscription follows your account. Your analytics id is a separate random id created on your phone for Mixpanel. Apple assigns its own transaction identifiers to purchases, which RevenueCat receives from Apple. We do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and we do not ask for tracking permission.

Diagnostics. The app has no crash-reporting service. Errors from routine generation are recorded as an analytics event with the error message, which never contains your health answers.

How your health data is used

When you generate a routine, your questionnaire answers are sent to our server, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API and returns the result to your phone. The request contains your health profile; it does not contain your name or email. Anthropic processes the request under its API data policy, which prohibits training on this data and sets its own limited retention period (see anthropic.com/privacy). Our server keeps your answers under the retention terms below. No other third party receives your health answers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your health answers for advertising.
  • We do not claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Retention and deletion

On your phone. If you leave the consultation without subscribing, your answers are kept for 30 days and then deleted, matching the promise made in the app. A finished profile that has been paid for stays on the phone until you delete your account or the app. Deleting the app removes everything on it immediately.

On our server. Your health profile and the routine built from it are kept for three days after you generate the routine. If you subscribe, they are kept for as long as your account exists; if you do not, they are deleted automatically. A subscriber's daily check-offs and account data are kept while the account exists.

Deleting everything. You can delete your account and every piece of data we hold about you from Settings in the app; deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. This removes your health profile, your routine, your daily check-offs, your account, and any findings-summary email that matches your account's address. It does not cancel a subscription, which is billed by Apple and cancelled in your Apple Account settings, and Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their terms. You can also ask us at cameron@sentic.io and we will complete it within 30 days, including any email you gave us under a different address.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to processing. Email cameron@sentic.io. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.
